# Your vision,  our expertise.

Specialists in full-cycle product development — blending human expertise with AI-driven automation to find you where you are.

## We    partner    with    founders    and    companies    to    design,    build,    and    scale    your    digital    products    —    combining    technical    depth,    execution    focus,    and    the    best    AI    tools    to    make    anything    real.

Who we are

Brilean is a digital product studio based in Europe. We work with ambitious teams across the world, including the U.S., and the UAE, delivering complex builds — with a lean, focused footprint.

We work in small, senior teams. Designers, engineers, and product thinkers — and now AI specialists — close to the problem, close to the result. No bloated decks. No handoffs for the sake of it. Just the right people, doing the work and AI handling the rest.

What we did

Used MuleSoft to streamline operations and improve data accessibility. Integrated crucial maritime data (ship schedules, truck turnaround times, container and reefer tracking, vessel and railway operations) from the customer’s database into Salesforce. Enabled real-time synchronization of this critical operational data.

iMPACT
